# 3 days in Lisbon with friends

Three days in Lisbon cost about €190 per person for 4 or 6 friends before the apartment, flights, dinners and evening drinks, and the apartment is the only line that changes with group size. All four of Lisbon's historic lifts are closed, and only the Graça funicular, opened in 2024, is in regular service (carris.pt, 30 Oct 2026), so this route goes up by funicular or tram and comes down on foot. Day 3 splits the group between Sintra and the beach, and everyone meets again for dinner. Start from our ready-made Portugal plan (Lisbon + Porto) in PlanVIA, the AI group trip planner: copy it and keep the 3 days that fit your group.

## How much do 3 days in Lisbon cost for 4 friends?

About €190 per person, excluding flights, the apartment, dinners and evening drinks, for 3 nights and 3 days on the ground with the Sintra half of day 3. The apartment adds 75% of one night's price per person for 4 friends (nightly price × 3 ÷ 4) and 50% for 6. As an illustration only, not a market price: a €200 apartment costs €150 each for 4 and €100 each for 6. Dinners and drinks are not in the total because we have no menu prices for them.

**Notes on the table**

- **Group size changes only the apartment.** Every other line is per person and the same for 4 or 6. If one of you is 24 or younger, Jerónimos and Belém Tower are half price for ages 13 to 24.
- **What the 24h ticket covers.** The "Bilhete 24h CARRIS/Metro" gives unlimited rides on the Metro and the whole Carris network, trams included, for 24 hours after you first validate it. Carris doesn't say whether it covers the Graça funicular, so the table adds the €4.30 on-board fare. For day 3 the "24h Carris/Metro/CP" ticket (€11.40) adds the CP trains to Sintra and Cascais. Sources: [Carris, conditions of carriage](https://carris.pt/media/3djlxq4q/condi%C3%A7%C3%B5es-gerais-transporte_vers%C3%A3ofinal_maio2025.pdf), [CP, 24h Carris/Metro/CP](https://www.cp.pt/info/pt/w/24-horas).
- **Weekday lunches.** The €18.50 set lunch (main course, drinks and coffee) runs Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays, for groups of up to 8. It is not offered on weekends, so the table keeps the same amount as an estimate for those days.
- **Airport to the apartment.** The Red line starts at Aeroporto (station open 06:30–01:00, trains every 6 to 10 minutes on weekdays; [Metro de Lisboa](https://www.metrolisboa.pt/viajar/horarios-e-frequencias/)). For Baixa-Chiado change at Alameda, and check the network map for your dates (see "Metro works" below). A 24h ticket validated at the airport covers the ride; a single ticket is €1.90. Since 15 October 2026, taxis that join a new city scheme charge fixed fares from the airport to central parishes such as Santa Maria Maior and Misericórdia: €19 on weekdays by day, €23 at night (22:00–07:00), on weekends and on holidays (reported by [DN](https://www.dn.pt/sociedade/tarifrio-fixo-dos-txis-no-aeroporto-de-lisboa-entrou-em-vigor-o-que-muda) and [DECO](https://www.deco.proteste.pt/familia-consumo/ferias-lazer/noticias/taxis-com-tarifa-fixa-aeroporto-lisboa), not confirmed by the city). A standard cab usually seats four, so 4 friends pay about €4.75 each in one cab and 6 friends about €6.33 each in two. Ride-hail apps are not on the fixed fare (reported by DN).
- **Metro works.** For the future Circular line, the Green line closed between Rossio and Cais do Sodré on 2–5 May 2026 and Cais do Sodré station closed on 8–26 August 2026. Metro de Lisboa expects the new Circular line to open in the first quarter of 2027, which will change how the Green line runs, so check the network map for your dates ([Metro de Lisboa, 27 Apr 2026](https://www.metrolisboa.pt/institucional/2026/04/27/encerramento-temporario-do-troco-rossio-cais-do-sodre-linha-verde/)).
- **Lisboa Card.** The 72h card costs €62, and the price is valid until 31 March 2027, so a May 2027 trip may see a new price. Pay as you go, the castle, Jerónimos, Belém Tower, two 24h tickets and the 24h ticket with trains come to €75.90, so the 72h card is about €14 cheaper if you do all three sights. Jerónimos and Belém Tower still need a booked time slot, and Pena Palace is discounted, not free. The card also covers the Sintra and Cascais trains. Source: [Turismo de Lisboa shop](https://shop.visitlisboa.com/collections/tourist-cards/products/lisboa-card), [Castelo de São Jorge, tickets](https://castelodesaojorge.pt/en/plan-your-visit/choose-your-ticket/).
- **Shared costs.** Put the apartment and group dinners into Splitwise or Tricount, both free. PlanVIA does not track who paid.

All prices are 2026 tariffs, checked on 30 Oct 2026. Re-check in March 2027.

## Where should a group of 4–6 stay in Lisbon?

Stay between Rossio and Cais do Sodré, because day 3 starts at one of them: Sintra trains leave from Rossio, and the Cascais line leaves from Cais do Sodré (Belém in 8 minutes, Carcavelos in about 27, Cascais in 40). Budget €12 each for tourist tax: €4 per night per guest over 13, up to 7 nights.

- **Baixa and Rossio.** Closest to the Sintra train, and Baixa sits between the two stations. Ask the host about night noise on the street side.
- **Cais do Sodré.** Closest to the Cascais line and to Belém, and one of the two bar areas, with Bairro Alto. If you sleep here, ask which side the bedrooms face.
- **Chiado and Bairro Alto.** The Santa Justa and Glória lifts used to be the shortcut up from Baixa and Restauradores. Both are closed, so count the uphill with suitcases, or take bus 51E for Glória.
- **Alfama.** Next to the castle and the Graça funicular, uphill from the river: the nearest lifts are the Graça funicular and the two lifts from Baixa that the castle's site lists (see day 1).

Search by bedrooms, not by guests: "sleeps 6" often means a sofa bed, and whoever gets it will remember that longer than the dinner.

Sources: [CP, Cascais line timetable](https://www.cp.pt/info/documents/d/cp/cascais-fds-6-abril) (valid from 6 April 2026), [Lisbon municipality](https://informacao.lisboa.pt/en/news/detail/tourist-tax-on-overnight-stays-rises-to-4-euros). Checked: Oct 2026.

## Which Lisbon lifts and funiculars are running in 2027?

One of five: the Graça funicular, which opened in 2024 and has run daily 07:00–21:00 since 15 May 2026. The four historic lifts, Santa Justa, Glória, Bica and Lavra, are all listed as "Encerrado temporariamente" (temporarily closed) on carris.pt with no reopening date, and a new Glória is reported for 2029 (RFM, 2 Sep 2026; reported, not confirmed). Plan 2027 without them and check carris.pt in the week you book.

## Day 1: Baixa, Alfama and Graça

Up by funicular, down on foot. The castle is €17 per adult (€8.50 for ages 13–25), open 09:00–21:00 from March to October with last entry at 20:30, and closed on 1 May.

1. **Morning: Mouraria to Graça.** Take the Graça funicular from Rua dos Lagares up to Largo da Graça (07:00–21:00, 14 people per trip). If it is shut, take tram 28E from Martim Moniz. The Graça viewpoint is the first stop.
2. **Midday: Castelo de São Jorge.** Buy tickets online on the castle's own platform, [CasteloSaoJorge.bol.pt](https://CasteloSaoJorge.bol.pt/), or at the ticket office. If you would rather walk up from Baixa, the castle's site lists two routes with lifts: the Castelo lift (Rua dos Fanqueiros 176 to Rua da Madalena) and the Chão do Loureiro car-park lift.
3. **Afternoon: down through Alfama to Baixa.** All downhill, so nobody needs a lift.
4. **Evening: Bairro Alto or Cais do Sodré.** Pick the area closest to where you sleep, so the friend who leaves at 23:00 can walk home. For a fado dinner, [Adega Machado](https://www.adegamachado.pt/en/gastronomia/) in Bairro Alto serves a €72 tasting menu (wine pairing +€30), with the kitchen open from 19:00 and live fado from 20:30. It is open daily, tables are few and evenings fill up well in advance, and group rules are not published: write or call before you tell the group chat it's booked.

Book before you go: castle tickets and the Adega Machado table.

Sources: [Castelo de São Jorge, tickets](https://castelodesaojorge.pt/en/plan-your-visit/choose-your-ticket/) and [useful information](https://castelodesaojorge.pt/en/information/useful-information/), [Carris, Graça funicular](https://www.carris.pt/descubra/abriu-o-funicular-da-graca/), [Adega Machado](https://www.adegamachado.pt/en/contactos/). Checked: Oct 2026.

## Day 2: Belém and LX Factory (Tuesday to Sunday only)

Jerónimos Monastery (€18) and Belém Tower (€15) cost €33 together and both are closed on Mondays. They open Tuesday to Sunday 09:30–17:30, and also close on 1 January, Easter Sunday, 1 May, 13 June and 25 December. If your day 1 is a Sunday, swap days 2 and 3.

1. **Getting there.** Tram 15E runs from Praça da Figueira through Cais do Sodré and Alcântara to Belém, with a stop at Mosteiro dos Jerónimos (€3.30 on board, or covered by the 24h ticket). The train from Cais do Sodré to Belém takes 8 minutes. The 15E has had temporary changes this autumn, so check [Carris service changes](https://www.carris.pt/viaje/alteracoes-de-servico/) before you go.
2. **Morning: Jerónimos.** The cloister is open 09:30–17:30 with last entry at 17:00. The church is open Tuesday to Saturday 10:30–17:00 and, on Sundays and religious holidays, 14:00–17:00. The ticket office closes at 16:30.
3. **Belém Tower.** It reopened to visitors on 27 May 2026 after conservation work, and visits now run by time slot. The press reports up to 60 entries every 30 minutes and about 900 visitors a day (reported, not confirmed by the monument). Book a slot for the whole group in one order.
4. **Pastéis de Belém.** €1.60 per pastry, €9.60 for six, espresso €1.20 (menu page without a date, so confirm at the counter).
5. **Afternoon: LX Factory in Alcântara,** on the same tram 15E line. Shops and restaurants keep their own hours.
6. **Evening: group dinner.** One table for everyone before the day-3 split.

Sources: [Museus e Monumentos de Portugal, Belém Tower](https://www.museusemonumentos.pt/pt/museus-e-monumentos/torre-de-belem), [Jerónimos](https://www.museusemonumentos.pt/pt/museus-e-monumentos/mosteiro-dos-jeronimos-e-capela-de-sao-jeronimo), [reopening notice, 20 May 2026](https://www.museusemonumentos.pt/pt/noticia-com/torre-de-belem-reabre-ao-publico), [Carris, tram 15E](https://www.carris.pt/viaje/carreiras/15e/), [Pastéis de Belém](https://pasteisdebelem.pt/menu/?lang=en), [Carris tariffs 2026](https://www.carris.pt/descubra/novo-tarifario-2026/), [DN, 25 May 2026 (media)](https://www.dn.pt/cultura/torre-de-belm-reabre-com-acesso-limitado-a-900-visitantes-por-dia), [Sábado/Lusa, 26 May 2026 (media)](https://www.sabado.pt/gps/amp/torre-de-belem-reabre-a-brilhar-e-com-novo-sistema-de-entradas-para-reduzir-filas). Checked: Oct 2026.

## Is 3 days enough for Lisbon and Sintra with a group?

Yes, if Sintra takes only half the group on day 3. The Sintra half needs most of a day: about 40 minutes each way by train, about 30 minutes from the Pena park entrance to the palace, and a palace slot that you can't arrive late for. The beach half is faster: Carcavelos is about 27 minutes from Cais do Sodré and Cascais 40, with trains every 20 minutes in the middle of the day.

### The PlanVIA rule: 70/30

About 70% of the programme is shared and about 30% each person spends as they like. In this route the shared 70% is day 1, day 2 and every dinner. The 30% is day 3, from breakfast until dinner. Settle the split the evening before: the Sintra people book the Pena slot, the beach people pick a train, and nobody has to defend their choice.

**Day 3, Sintra half (about €31, plus the 24h ticket with trains)**

- **Train from Rossio to Sintra.** About 40 minutes. Buy CP's 24h Carris/Metro/CP ticket (€11.40 plus the €0.50 card): it covers the Sintra and Cascais trains, the Metro, and Carris buses and trams for 24 hours. CP's Train&Bus day ticket (€14 plus €0.50 card) is listed as temporarily suspended.
- **Pena Palace.** The Essential visit (palace and park) is €20 per adult. The park alone is €12 and the Full Tour is €45, so the group does not have to buy the same ticket. You enter the palace only at the booked date and time: arrive early and you wait, arrive late and you are refused without a refund. Online tickets can be moved to another date and time within one year, but only before your slot: a missed slot can't be changed or refunded. Buy all tickets in one order for one slot, and set the meeting point at the park entrance 30 minutes before it.
- **Moorish Castle.** €12, on its own ticket: the Pena tickets don't include it.
- **Getting up to the monuments.** Bus 434 runs from Sintra station to the Moorish Castle and Pena: €10.96 for a 24-hour ticket that also covers bus 435 (Parques de Sintra). In summer 2026 the municipality restricted traffic in Sintra's historic centre from 8 July to 8 September, with ride-hail pick-ups only at 12 points, Pena among them (reported by RTP, not confirmed by the council). The 2027 rules are not published, so plan on train and bus, not on a car.

**Day 3, beach half (cheaper)**

- **Carcavelos.** Train from Cais do Sodré, get off at Carcavelos station and walk to the beach. It has a restaurant, a bar and a windsurfing school. The 24h Carris/Metro/CP ticket (€11.40) covers the train. In 2026 Carcavelos had lifeguards from 1 May to 30 September; the 2027 dates are set each spring.

## What do friends flying in from the UK need to know about border checks in 2027?

Only friends with non-EU passports (a UK passport, for example) are registered in the EU Entry/Exit System, which has been fully operational since 10 April 2026: passport scan, a face image and fingerprints at the first crossing. ETIAS is not in operation and has no start date (European Commission, checked 30 Oct 2026); the EU promises several months' notice, so check again when you book. Once it starts it costs €20 per person, and under-18s and over-70s don't pay. In a mixed group, friends with EU passports usually clear the border first: agree a meeting point after passport control. More in [/travel-alerts](/travel-alerts) [страница ещё не опубликована, W2-03].

Sources: [European Commission, EES](https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/policies/schengen/smart-borders/entry-exit-system_en), [ETIAS](https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/policies/schengen/smart-borders/european-travel-information-authorisation-system_en).
