# National rail strike in France on Thursday 5 November

**What changed:** France's rail unions have called a national strike at SNCF for Thursday 5 November 2026. SUD-Rail and UNSA-Ferroviaire have published the joint call, and the media name CGT-Cheminots and CFDT-Cheminots as co-signatories. The unions are demanding higher pay, more jobs and better working conditions. SNCF has not yet published which trains will run.

**What it means for your group:**

- **Trains at risk:** TGV INOUI, Intercités, TER and Paris-region trains could all run a reduced service (reported by the media, 22–23 October).
- **Thursday-to-Sunday trips:** if your group planned to leave on Thursday 5 November, the outbound day is the one at risk. The rail unions' call covers only 5 November, but the CGT confederation has called to spread strikes "on 5 November and after" (reported, not confirmed).
- **Not just trains:** 5 November is also a nationwide day of strikes and protests called by several union confederations, including the CGT and CFDT. Schools and public services may be disrupted and a march is expected in Paris (reported, not confirmed).
- **Paris metro:** as of 23 October there was no RATP strike notice for 5 November (reported). The metro may run normally even if RER and Transilien trains don't. The CGT's wider strike call could still change that.
- **Late notice:** the full list of cancelled trains usually comes out the day before, which leaves little time to rebook six people.

**What to do:**

1. If you can, move the trip to Wednesday 4 or Friday 6 November now, while seats are still available, but note that strike hours aren't published yet, so the evening of 4 November and the morning of 6 November may also be affected, and check your fare's exchange conditions first.
2. If you stay on 5 November, check SNCF Connect on 4 November and have a plan B ready: a later train, a coach, or a rental car if 4–5 of you can share the cost and the drive. The media expect busier roads that day.
3. Keep tickets for everyone on one booking where you can, so a single rebooking moves the whole group.
4. Before travelling through Paris, check RATP and Île-de-France Mobilités the day before.
