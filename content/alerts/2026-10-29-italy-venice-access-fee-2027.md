# Venice day-tripper fee 2027: 72 days from 26 March to 15 August, up to €10 per person

**What changed:** The City of Venice has approved the 2027 calendar for the access fee that day visitors pay to enter the historic centre. It runs on 72 days from Good Friday, 26 March, to 15 August 2027, mostly Friday to Sunday. It also covers Easter Monday and two continuous blocks, 23 April–2 May and 28 May–6 June. The fee applies from 08:30 to 16:30, half an hour longer than in 2026, and costs up to €10 per person. Exemptions, booking and payment rules for 2027 will be set later, and the city will announce when the booking portal opens.

**What it means for your group:**

- **Cost of a weekend day trip:** a group of 6 visiting on a Saturday in May could pay up to €60.
- **Free days:** Monday to Thursday is fee-free all season, except Easter Monday (29 March), 26–29 April and 31 May–3 June.
- **Evening visits:** the fee covers 08:30–16:30, so an evening-only visit falls outside it.
- **Staying overnight (2026 rules):** guests in accommodation that charges Venice's tourist tax were exempt, but had to request the exemption. Children under 14 were exempt with ID. The 2027 rules may differ.
- **Early booking (2026 rules):** paying by the fourth day before your visit cost €5, and paying later cost €10. For 6 friends that was a €30 difference.
- **Islands (2026 rules):** Lido, Murano, Burano and Torcello were not charged in 2026.

**What to do:**

1. Check your Venice day against the 2027 calendar before you fix the itinerary. If you can, choose a Monday to Thursday outside the blocks above.
2. If you sleep in Venice, plan to request the exemption once the 2027 rules are out, not to pay.
3. If you go on a fee day, every friend needs their own access code, so don't leave it to one person at the station.
4. Watch for the 2027 booking portal opening, because paying early was cheaper in 2026.
