# Ouigo train drivers' strike in Spain: 30 Oct, 2, 6 and 9 Nov, 4 and 8 Dec

**What changed:** The drivers' union SEMAF has called strikes at Ouigo España on six days of 2026: 24-hour strikes on 30 October and 2 November, and partial strikes on 6 and 9 November and on 4 and 8 December. Spain's Ministry of Transport set minimum services on 27 October. For 30 October and 2 November, Ouigo says 55 of 77 scheduled trains will run (71%).

**What it means for your group:**

- **The dates are long weekends:** All Saints (30 Oct–2 Nov), Almudena in Madrid (6–9 Nov) and the December bridge (4–8 Dec), which is when groups of friends travel.
- **Cancelled on Friday 30 October:** 12 Ouigo trains, including Madrid-Atocha 06:55 to Seville, Madrid-Chamartín 07:15 to Valencia, 08:15 to Murcia, 13:15 to Valencia and 16:15 to Murcia, Madrid-Atocha 17:35 to Málaga, Seville 10:37 to Madrid and Málaga 21:06 to Madrid.
- **Cancelled on Monday 2 November:** 10 Ouigo trains, including Madrid-Atocha 06:55 to Seville, Madrid-Chamartín 07:15 to Valencia, 08:15 to Murcia, Madrid-Atocha 09:55 to Málaga, Málaga 13:41 to Madrid and Murcia 19:53 to Madrid. The full list is on Ouigo's strike page.
- **Partial strike days:** strike hours of 06:00–12:00 and 18:00–23:59 on 6 and 9 November and 4 and 8 December are reported, not confirmed: they are not on the Ministry's or Ouigo's pages yet, and Ouigo had not published train lists for those days as of 28 October.
- **Only Ouigo is affected:** the minimum-services decision covers Ouigo only. Renfe and Iryo are not part of this strike.
- **One booking, one inbox:** Ouigo emails affected passengers at the address on the booking. If one friend booked for all six of you, only that friend gets the email.

**What to do:**

1. Whoever made the booking: check your email and Ouigo's strike page now, and forward anything to the group.
2. If your train is cancelled, Ouigo offers a free ticket change or a refund. Decide as a group and rebook everyone together, because the remaining trains fill up first.
3. For 6 and 9 November and 4 and 8 December, watch the same page for the train lists. If you can, pick a train between 12:00 and 18:00, outside the reported (not confirmed) strike hours.
4. If your dates are fixed, consider a refundable backup on Renfe or Iryo for the busiest legs.
