# Italy strike weekend: general strike on 20 November, rail and air strikes on 20–22 November

**What changed:** On 21 October Italy's largest union, CGIL, proclaimed a national general strike for Friday 20 November 2026. It covers the entire working day in all public and private sectors, and the transport unions will announce exact hours later. Italian media, citing the Ministry of Transport's strike calendar, also list a national rail strike called by CUB Trasporti and SGB Ferrovie from 21:00 on Friday 20 November to 20:00 on Saturday 21 November, and a national air traffic control (ENAV) strike from 13:00 to 17:00 on Saturday 21 November. The rail and air strikes are reported, not confirmed.

**What it means for your group:**

- **A Friday-to-Sunday city break on 20–22 November is the most exposed plan:** the outbound Friday and most of Saturday may have fewer trains, flights, buses and metros.
- **Also reported:**
  - local transport strikes on 21 November, including GTT in Turin and EAV around Naples;
  - an all-day ENAV strike at Milan Malpensa on Sunday 22 November;
  - a security staff strike at Venice Marco Polo airport on 22 November.
- **What still runs:** during strikes Trenitalia guarantees regional trains from 06:00 to 09:00 and 18:00 to 21:00 on working days, plus a published list of long-distance trains (Table A).
- **Dates can change:** strikes in Italy are sometimes revoked or postponed, so the picture can shift until a few days before.

**What to do:**

1. If your dates are flexible, shift the trip a week (27–29 November) or travel out on Thursday 19 November.
2. If you can't move: book trains that leave inside the guaranteed windows or appear in Trenitalia's Table A, and avoid flights departing between 13:00 and 17:00 on 21 November.
3. Have everyone check their train or airline app 1–2 days before. Italo publishes its own list of guaranteed trains.
4. For getting around town on 20–21 November, plan walkable days and set aside money for taxis. 4 friends sharing a taxi pay a quarter each.
