# Barcelona tourist tax: up to €12 per person per night, and €1 more from April 2027

**What changed:** Since 1 April 2026, Catalonia's regional tourist tax has doubled in Barcelona and the city's surcharge has risen from €4 to €5 per person per night. The city surcharge goes up to €6 on 1 April 2027 and €7 on 1 April 2028, with a cap of €8.

**What it means for your group:**

Total per person per night from 1 April 2026 (regional tax plus city surcharge):

| Where you sleep | Per person per night |
|---|---|
| 5-star hotel | €12.00 |
| 4-star hotel | €8.40 |
| Tourist apartment (HUT) | €9.50 |
| Other accommodation | €7.00 |

- **Example:** 6 friends in a tourist apartment for 4 nights pay 6 × 4 × €9.50 = €228, or €38 each, on top of the rent.
- **From April 2027:** the city surcharge alone adds €1 per person per night, so the same trip costs €24 more. That assumes the regional part stays the same.
- **Long stays:** the tax is charged for a maximum of 7 consecutive nights.
- **Under-17s:** exempt.
- **Budget gaps:** if budgets in the group differ, the tax widens the gap. In a 5-star hotel it's €12 a night per person, against €7 in other accommodation.

**What to do:**

1. Add the tax to the per-person budget before your group picks accommodation. It is easy to miss when comparing listings.
2. Ask the host or hotel whether the price you see already includes it.
3. For a trip after 1 April 2027, add €1 per person per night to the table above.
4. If someone in the group is under 17, mention it at check-in.
