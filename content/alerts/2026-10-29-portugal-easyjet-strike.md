# easyJet cabin crew strike in Portugal, 19–23 December

**What changed:** The cabin crew union SNPVAC has called a strike by easyJet cabin crew based in Lisbon, Porto and Faro from Saturday 19 to Wednesday 23 December 2026. It is the second strike after one on 2–6 October, when the union expected about 500 flights to be affected and later said about 370 were cancelled. The Portuguese government has set minimum services for both strike periods. easyJet has not published a list of cancelled December flights (reported by The Portugal News on 28 October).

**What it means for your group:**

- **Christmas trips are hit:** flights that start or end in Lisbon, Porto or Faro and are crewed from those bases are at risk on the last weekend before Christmas. The union expects a bigger impact than in October because of holiday demand.
- **Islands too:** Madeira and Porto Santo are not strike bases, but their flights from Lisbon and Porto can still be cancelled (reported).
- **Your rights if cancelled:**
  - You choose between a refund, re-routing at the earliest opportunity, or re-routing at a later date.
  - easyJet must also cover meals and, if needed, a hotel while you wait.
  - Under EU rules, a strike by the airline's own staff is not an "extraordinary circumstance". You can therefore claim €250, €400 or €600 per person, depending on distance, unless you were told more than 14 days before departure, or easyJet offered a replacement flight close to your original times (Your Europe lists the exact windows). For a 19 December flight: if you're told on 5 December or later, you may be owed compensation; if you're told on 4 December or earlier, you're not.
- **One booking, one inbox:** the friend who booked for everyone gets the notices.

**What to do:**

1. If you haven't booked yet, look at other airlines or at 18 or 24 December for Portugal flights.
2. If you're already booked on easyJet for 19–23 December, the booker should check the app and email daily from early December and forward updates to the group.
3. If your flight is cancelled, choose re-routing quickly and rebook the whole group at once, because seats on 6 tickets disappear faster than on 1.
4. Keep receipts for meals and hotels. File any claim with easyJet first. If you get no answer within 2 months, or you disagree with it, go to ANAC, Portugal's aviation authority.
