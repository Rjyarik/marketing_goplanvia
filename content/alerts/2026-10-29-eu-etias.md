# ETIAS isn't live yet and has no start date: don't pay anyone for it now

**What changed:** Nothing yet. The European Commission says ETIAS "is currently not in operation", that no applications are being collected, and that the EU will announce the start date "several months prior to its launch". The earlier target of late 2026 is no longer on the Commission's ETIAS page, and as of 19 October 2026 no date had been published (reported by SchengenVisaInfo).

**What it means for your group:**

- **Trips in the next few months:** no ETIAS is needed. The EU has promised several months' notice before launch and has not given it yet. For trips later in 2027, check again before you fly.
- **Once it starts:** friends with passports from the 59 visa-exempt countries (for example the US, UK, Canada and Australia) will each need their own ETIAS for short stays (up to 90 days in any 180) in 30 European countries.
- **Cost once live:** €20 per person; under-18s and over-70s pay nothing. For 4 adult friends that is €80.
- **Boarding:** airlines and ferry operators will check each traveller's ETIAS within 48 hours before departure, so a missing one will be a problem at check-in, not only at the border.
- **Friends who already need a Schengen visa** don't need ETIAS. They keep applying for a visa as before.

**What to do:**

1. Don't apply or pay now. Since the EU is not collecting applications, any website taking ETIAS money today is not the official system.
2. Save the official EU page and check it when you book trips for later in 2027. We will update this alert when a date is announced.
3. For mixed groups: when ETIAS launches, have each visa-exempt friend apply themselves with their own passport details, then confirm in the group chat that everyone has theirs before you fly.
